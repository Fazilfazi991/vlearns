Course Overview

HACCP Level 4 is an advanced management-level program created for professionals who want to lead food safety systems, manage HACCP implementation, conduct audits, and strengthen compliance. The course is suitable for learners aiming for leadership roles in food safety, QA/QC, compliance, and auditing.

Who Should Attend

  • QA/QC and food safety professionals
  • HACCP team leaders
  • Managers and supervisors
  • Professionals aiming for leadership roles in MNCs
  • Graduates seeking advanced specialization in food safety

What You Will Learn

  • Advanced HACCP system design
  • Detailed hazard analysis and risk assessment
  • Validation and verification techniques
  • Food Safety Management Systems
  • Internal auditing and compliance
  • Leadership in food safety

Key Benefits

  • Internationally accredited certification recognized globally
  • Enhance leadership skills and advance your career
  • Ensure compliance and reduce operational risks
  • Increase employability and open doors to global opportunities
